What is Blackbird?

321Blackbird is a 3rd Wave Language Technology Platform (LTP) purpose-built for multilingual workflow orchestration and AI-driven process automation.

124443The 1st Wave of Language Technology Platforms was centered around CAT tools, with translation memory, fuzzy matching, and term bases as the core technologies. These platforms were visually defined by their “left-pane/right-pane” user interfaces.

355521The 2nd Wave introduced business management features, cloud deployment, machine translation plugins, and connectors. However, these platforms remained fundamentally linear and still revolved around the CAT tool and its legacy UI — their underlying architecture remained largely unchanged.
